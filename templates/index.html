What is ATS and why does it matter?

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software that 99% of Fortune 500 companies use to filter resumes. It scans for keywords from the job description and ranks candidates. If your resume doesn't contain those keywords, it gets rejected automatically — even if you're qualified.
