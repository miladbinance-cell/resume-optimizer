Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

ResumeAI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use our service:

  • Resume files you upload (PDF, DOCX, TXT)
  • Job descriptions you paste
  • Email address (for delivering results)
  • Payment information (processed by Stripe — we never store your card details)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Optimize your resume using AI
  • Deliver your optimized documents
  • Process payments
  • Improve our service

3. Data Retention

Your resume files are automatically deleted from our servers within 24 hours of processing. We do not sell, share, or use your resume data for any purpose other than providing the service you requested.

4. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We use Stripe for payment processing — your card data never touches our servers.
