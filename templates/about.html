About ResumeAI

ResumeAI was built with a simple mission: help job seekers get past ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) and land more interviews.

We know that 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS before a human ever sees them. That's why we built an AI-powered tool that analyzes job descriptions and rewrites your resume to match exactly what employers are looking for.

Our AI uses advanced natural language processing to identify key skills, requirements, and keywords from job descriptions, then naturally integrates them into your resume — making it both ATS-friendly and compelling to human readers.

We believe everyone deserves a fair shot at their dream job, regardless of whether they know how to "beat the system." That's why we keep our pricing simple and transparent — no subscriptions, no hidden fees.
