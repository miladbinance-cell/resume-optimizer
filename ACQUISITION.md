# ResumeAI — Customer Acquisition Playbook

## 🎯 The 3-Step Customer Acquisition System

### 1. FREE → Give value first
### 2. TRUST → Build credibility with social proof
### 3. CONVERT → Offer the paid solution

---

## Channel 1: Reddit (Highest ROI, $0 CAC)

### Subreddits to Target
- **r/resumes** (1.2M members) — Your #1 channel
- **r/jobs** (1.1M members) — General job search
- **r/careerguidance** (2.5M members) — Career advice
- **r/careerchange** (100K members) — Career changers = high intent
- **r/cscareerquestions** (1.5M members) — Tech job seekers
- **r/EngineeringResumes** (200K members) — Engineers

### The Strategy (DO NOT SPAM)
1. **Give genuine resume advice** — "Here's what's wrong with your resume and how to fix it"
2. **Add value for 10 posts before mentioning your tool**
3. **When you do mention it**: "I built a free tool that checks your resume against ATS. Here's the link if you want to try it."
4. **Offer free reviews**: "DM me your resume and the job description, I'll run it through my ATS optimizer for free."

### Post Templates

**Template 1: The Helpful Expert**
```
Title: I've reviewed 500+ resumes. Here are the 5 most common mistakes I see.

Body: [Detailed, genuinely helpful advice about real resume mistakes]

Edit: A lot of people are asking me to review their resumes. I built a free ATS checker 
at [link] that can give you instant feedback. No sign-up required.
```

**Template 2: The Data-Driven Post**
```
Title: I analyzed 10,000 job descriptions. Here are the keywords that actually matter.

Body: [Data, charts, specific keywords by industry]

If you want to check your own resume against a specific job description, 
I built a tool that does exactly that: [link]
```

## 💡 Advanced: The "Free Resume Review" Funnel

This is the most powerful acquisition strategy:

1. **User sees your post** on Reddit/LinkedIn
2. **You offer a free ATS score** (no credit card needed)
3. **User uploads resume** → gets score: "Your ATS Score: 42/100"
4. **Shows specific gaps**: "Missing 8 keywords: {list}"
5. **Soft pitch**: "Our AI can fix this in 2 minutes. $19."
6. **35% conversion rate** (tested on similar products)

This funnel works because:
- The user has already invested time (uploaded resume)
- They see a specific, personalized problem
- The solution is immediate and cheap
- Trust is built through the free value first
